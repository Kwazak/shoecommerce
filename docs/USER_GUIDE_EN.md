# User Guide — Pabrik Sepatu Karya Jaya 1978

Welcome to **Karya Jaya 1978**, an Indonesian footwear workshop in Cibaduyut, Bandung, crafting durable everyday shoes, sandals, and work boots directly from the factory bench to your doorstep—with honest pricing and no middleman markup (*Kualitas Asli Buatan Pabrik*).

---

## 1. Guide for Retail Shoppers (B2C)

Whether you are buying one pair for school, daily work, or outdoor errands, ordering directly from the factory is quick and easy.

### Browsing & Checking Real-Time Stock
1. Open the **Katalog Pabrik** (Factory Catalog) from the top menu.
2. Narrow down your search by:
   - **Category:** *Sepatu* (Shoes), *Sandal* (Sandals), or *Boot* (Boots).
   - **Size:** Indonesian standard sizes **38 through 44**.
   - **Price Range**, **Keyword**, or **"Hanya stok siap kirim"** (Ready-to-ship stock only).
3. Click any product to see its materials (upper, sole, stitching), color choices, and exact remaining warehouse stock for each size.

### Measuring Your Foot at Home (1:1 Printable Foot Ruler)
To avoid ordering the wrong size:
1. On any product page, click **"Cetak Penggaris Ukur Kaki 1:1"** next to the size chart.
2. Print the page on A4 paper at **100% / Actual Size** (do not choose "Fit to Page").
3. Measure the **50 mm × 50 mm** verification square with a regular ruler to confirm your printer scale is accurate.
4. Place your heel against the **23.0 cm** reference line and read the size marker (**38 to 44**) at the tip of your longest toe.

### Checkout, Payment & Your 90-Day Warranty Coupon
1. Select your preferred color, size, and quantity, then click **"Masukkan ke Laci Kasir"** (Add to Cash Drawer).
2. You can shop as a **guest** or create a free **Pembeli Eceran (B2C)** account under **Daftar Mitra/Pembeli** to keep track of all your past orders.
3. Click **Laci Kasir** → **Buat Faktur** (Create Invoice), enter your shipping address, and choose:
   - **Delivery:** Regular Courier (*Kurir Reguler*).
   - **Payment:**
     - **Pembayaran Online** (QRIS, Virtual Account, or Alfamart/Indomaret).
     - **COD (Bayar di Tempat)** — pay cash to the courier when your package arrives.
4. After submitting, your **Official Factory Invoice (*Faktur Penjualan Resmi*)** appears.
   - If paying online, complete payment within **24 hours** using the **Bayar Sekarang** button (or the local test simulator button when testing).
   - Click **"Cetak Faktur (Print)"** to print or save your invoice along with the tear-off **90-Day Sole & Stitching Warranty Coupon (*Kupon Garansi Lem & Jahitan 90 Hari*)** at the bottom.

## 2. Guide for Wholesale Shoe Shops & Resellers (B2B)

We partner with regional shoe shops, market stalls, and project suppliers across Indonesia.

### Registering & Getting Approved as a B2B Partner
1. Click **Daftar Mitra/Pembeli** and select **Mitra Toko / Grosir (B2B)**.
2. Fill in your personal details along with your **Shop Name (*Nama Toko*)**, **City/Regency (*Kota/Kabupaten*)**, and **Full Shop Address (*Alamat Lengkap Toko*)**.
3. Once registered, your account shows a **"Menunggu Verifikasi Admin Pabrik"** (Awaiting Factory Admin Verification) notice.
4. As soon as our Factory Admin approves your shop, your account automatically unlocks **wholesale kodi discounts** and **14-Day Credit Terms**.

### How Tiered Kodi Discounts Work
Discounts are calculated automatically in your **Laci Kasir** based on the **total number of pairs** in your order (you can mix models, colors, and sizes):

| Wholesale Tier | Total Pairs | Automatic Factory Discount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **½ Kodi** | 6 – 11 pairs | **15% OFF** |
| **1 Kodi** | 12 – 23 pairs | **25% OFF** |
| **≥ 2 Kodi** | 24+ pairs | **35% OFF** |

*Tip:* Not yet approved, or just browsing? Turn on **"Simulasi Harga Kodian B2B"** on the catalog or product page to preview wholesale prices right away.

### Ordering Fast with the Size-Series Matrix & Starter Bundles
- **Size-Series Ledger Matrix (*Matriks Buku Kas Seri Ukuran*):**
  When signed in as an approved B2B partner (or in B2B simulation mode), scroll down on any product page to see the full size-by-color ledger table (`38–44`).
  - Click **"Isi 1 Seri Standar (39–43)"** to fill 1 pair in each standard size (`39, 40, 41, 42, 43`) in one click, or type your own quantities.
  - Click **"Masukkan Semua ke Keranjang Grosir"** to add the entire series at once.
- **Starter Business Bundles (*Paket Usaha Pemula*):**
  On the Home and Catalog pages, browse ready-made bundles (such as *Paket Buka Lapak ½ Kodi Sandal Harian* or *Paket Proyek 6 Pasang Boot Karet*) and click **"Pesan Paket Usaha"** to add the full bundle in one click.
- **Bulk Shipping & 14-Day Credit:**
  At checkout, approved B2B partners can choose **Ekspedisi Kargo Truk** (economical bulk truck freight) and **Tempo Mitra B2B (14 Hari)** (goods ship immediately; settle payment within 14 days of the invoice date).

## 3. Quick-Start Guide for Factory Admin & Warehouse Staff

### Factory Admin Panel
1. Sign in via **Masuk** using your Factory Admin account (`admin@pabrik.id`) and open **Panel Admin Pabrik** from your profile menu.
2. **Sales Dashboard (*Ikhtisar Penjualan*):** Filter by *Hari Ini*, *7 Hari Terakhir*, *30 Hari Terakhir*, or *Semua Waktu* to review total revenue, B2C vs. B2B share, pairs sold by category, top-selling sizes (`38–44`), **Low-Stock Alerts** (`< 5` pairs), and **JATUH TEMPO** alerts for B2B credit invoices nearing their 14-day due date.
3. **Approving New Shops (*Verifikasi Mitra B2B*):** Review pending shop applications and click **"Setujui Mitra B2B"** to activate their wholesale pricing and 14-day credit.
4. **Catalog & Photos (*Katalog & Stok Gudang*):** Add new footwear models, upload product photos (`.jpg`, `.png`, `.webp`, up to 3 MB), update prices, add new colorways, or adjust warehouse physical stock per size.
5. **Orders & WhatsApp Log (*Faktur & Pengiriman*):** Search invoices, update order statuses, mark Tempo/COD invoices as paid (**[Tandai Lunas]**), run **"Jalankan Pengecekan Jatuh Tempo Sekarang"** to expire overdue unpaid orders, and check the **Log Pesan WhatsApp** drawer at the bottom.

### Warehouse Dispatch Station (*Scan Gudang & Resi*)
1. Open **Scan Gudang & Resi** from the Admin sidebar on a warehouse phone or packing PC.
2. **Step 1:** Scan the invoice barcode or type the invoice number (e.g., `FKJ-20261120-XXXX`) and press **Enter**.
3. **Step 2:** Check off each pair on the on-screen packing checklist as you place them in the box.
4. **Step 3:** Enter the **Courier Name (*Nama Ekspedisi*)** and **Tracking Number (*Nomor Resi*)**, then tap **"SAHKAN PENGIRIMAN & KIRIM NOTIFIKASI"**. The order is marked **Dikirim**, a **"TERKIRIM"** stamp appears, and the customer receives a WhatsApp dispatch notice.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

- **Why was my unpaid order automatically cancelled/expired?**
  Online payment orders (*Menunggu Pembayaran*) reserve warehouse stock for **24 hours**. If payment is not completed within 24 hours, the order automatically changes to **Kedaluwarsa** and the reserved pairs are released back to other buyers.
- **How do I check my order status?**
  Sign in and open **Riwayat Pesanan & Faktur** from your profile menu in the top bar. If you checked out as a guest, bookmark the unique invoice link shown right after checkout.
- **How do I claim the 90-day warranty?**
  Keep your printed invoice and the **Kupon Garansi Lem & Jahitan 90 Hari** attached at the bottom. If the sole separates or factory stitching comes loose under normal wear within 90 days of purchase, contact our factory team with your invoice number.
- **How do I contact the factory?**
  Visit or reach out to **Pabrik Alas Kaki Karya Jaya**, Jl. Raya Cibaduyut No. 78, Bandung, Jawa Barat (Warehouse hours: Monday–Saturday, 08.00–17.00 WIB).
